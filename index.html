A DESIGN QUESTION

Can a traveler with no working
local SIM get online?

A click, a form, an SMS code and a trip to a passport kiosk ask different things of someone who has just landed. The design opportunity is to make basic connectivity easy to reach—and to explain any fallback before the traveler needs it.

This is a usability observation. These examples do not establish why an airport chose a policy or what any jurisdiction legally requires.

03 / READ THE EVIDENCE

A comparison you
can check.

Every airport card links directly to its evidence. Review date: 27 September 2026. A page being accessible today does not make its contents newly published.

How the sources were classified

Official passenger-service instructions are the primary evidence for DEL, HYD, SIN, SFO and LHR. DXB’s one-click description is from a dated official announcement; a current government page supports free availability only. TLV’s specific sign-in claim is from an explicitly unofficial guide, while the official maintenance notice establishes only that Wi-Fi exists.

What this comparison does—and does not—measure

“Friction” here means the kind of dependency: a click, registration, receiving an SMS, or locating a document-check facility. There is no numerical score and no measured connection time. Network reliability, speed, privacy practices, accessibility in practice and SMS delivery were not tested. These seven airports are not a representative sample of entire countries.

Visual examples and attribution

All journey diagrams on this page are original explanatory graphics based on the linked sources. They are labeled illustrations, not screenshots or reproductions of airport interfaces. No third-party portal screenshots, airport logos or photographs are redistributed. For official kiosk photos, see Hyderabad’s Wi-Fi page; those photos remain on the airport’s site.

Corrections to common shortcuts

Do not collapse these findings into “India requires an Indian number” or “all the others are one click.” HYD lists a country-code selector; LHR requires registration. A passport fallback is documented for DEL and HYD, but a boarding pass is not listed on their cited Wi-Fi pages. Old Changi kiosk examples are not evidence of today’s process. TLV remains unverified.
